# Live Presence & Human Decisions

Clado keeps humans effortlessly in control through **SSE live events**, **restrained AI-working feedback**, and **native `ask_user` decision cards**:

- **SSE Live Updates & Follow-AI Glide**: Toggle `Follow` to glide smoothly (`280ms`) to whatever card AI is inspecting or editing, or turn it off to browse freely.
- **≥80% Feathered AI-Working Glow**: Quiet cards and normal clicks stay strictly neutral monochrome. Only while AI is actively reading, editing, or streaming does a feathered ≥80% bottom-center breathing aura appear (shifting to top-center on mobile Bottom Sheets).
- **Structured `ask_user` Decision Cards**: When encountering design ambiguity, the Agent pauses with 2–4 selectable options plus custom input, highlights the related card in warm amber glow, and resumes seamlessly after your decision.
